07 — Frequently asked

Questions people actually ask.

Short, honest answers on how we work, what it costs, and what to expect after launch.

What kind of AI systems do you build? +
Voice agents that handle calls, custom chatbots, appointment setters, workflow automations that connect your tools, and full custom AI SaaS platforms. If it involves language models, agents, or automating repetitive work — we build it.
How long does an AI project take? +
A working voice agent or simple automation ships in 2-3 weeks. Custom multi-agent systems and integrated SaaS builds take 6-12 weeks depending on scope. We share a clear timeline before starting so nothing is a surprise.
Do I need technical knowledge to work with you? +
No. You describe the outcome you want; we handle the technical side. We speak in plain business language, share progress in demos rather than code, and only get technical when it saves you time.
How much does an AI system cost? +
It depends on scope. Focused automations start in the low thousands; production voice agents typically run mid-four to low-five figures; full custom platforms are quoted per project. We give a firm quote after the discovery call — no hourly guessing.
Can you integrate with our existing tools? +
Yes. We integrate with CRMs (HubSpot, Salesforce, Pipedrive), calendars (Google, Outlook), phone systems (Twilio, Vonage), Slack, WhatsApp, Notion, Airtable and most APIs. If it has an API or webhook, we can wire it up.
What happens after launch — do you provide support? +
Every build includes 30 days of post-launch support to iron out edge cases. After that, most clients keep us on a monthly retainer for monitoring, prompt tuning, and adding new capabilities as their team learns what works.
How do you handle data privacy and security? +
Your data stays in your infrastructure whenever possible. We use enterprise-tier LLM providers with zero-data-retention agreements, sign NDAs before discovery, and can deploy on-premises or in your own cloud for HIPAA, SOC2 or GDPR-sensitive workloads.
